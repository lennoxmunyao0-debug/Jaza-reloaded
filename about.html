About Us

Built for Kenya's Drivers

We're on a mission to make finding fuel as easy as ordering a ride. No queues, no guesswork — just fuel, fast.

Our Mission

Fuel Access for Every Kenyan Driver

RS PREMIUM was founded in Nairobi with one goal: eliminate the frustration of searching for fuel. We connect drivers to real-time station data — availability, prices, and locations — so every trip to the pump is predictable, efficient, and stress-free.

Whether you're a daily commuter in Westlands or a long-haul trucker on the Nairobi–Mombasa highway, RS PREMIUM gives you the information and tools to keep moving.

Our Vision

Africa's Leading Fuel Intelligence Platform

We envision a future where no driver across Africa has to wonder whether a station is open, stocked, or affordable. Starting in Kenya, we are building the infrastructure to bring real-time fuel intelligence to every major market on the continent.

Our platform supports stations and drivers equally — creating a transparent, data-driven fuel ecosystem that benefits everyone.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Reliability

We only list verified stations. Every data point on our platform is checked and updated in real time.

Speed

Time matters on the road. We optimise every interaction so you find fuel and get moving — fast.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no sponsored rankings. Stations are sorted purely by distance and availability.

Community

We're built for Kenyans, by Kenyans. Driver feedback shapes every feature we ship.

Our Journey

From Idea to
Kenya's Fuel Platform

A timeline of milestones that shaped RS PREMIUM into what it is today.

22
2022

The Idea is Born

During a fuel shortage in Nairobi, our founder spent 3 hours searching for an open station. That frustration became the seed of RS PREMIUM.

23
2023

Beta Launch in Nairobi

We launched a beta with 120 stations across Nairobi and onboarded our first 2,000 drivers in 60 days.

24
2024

National Expansion

Expanded to all 47 counties. Crossed 500 partner stations and 50,000 registered users. Launched real-time price tracking.

25
2025

Mobile App & Bookings

Launched the RS PREMIUM mobile app with fuel booking, push notifications, and digital receipts. Hit 95,000+ happy drivers.

26
2026

Today — 1,200+ Stations

Over 1,200 partner stations nationwide, real-time stock data, and eyes set on expansion to Uganda and Tanzania.
